The MrCharles DIM Wishlist

Your one stop shop for identifying optimal weapon rolls in Destiny Item Manager.

All data based off [Aegis](https://linktr.ee/TheAegisRelic)'s spreadsheets for [PvE Endgame Analysis](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JM-0SlxVDAi-C6rGVlLxa-J1WGewEeL8Qvq4htWZHhY/edit?usp=sharing).

## How to Use
Copy a Link

In DIM go to Settings->Wish List

Clear the wishlist of the current included rolls (If you want)

Under "Add another wish list" paste the link

## How it works
Unlike some other lists, this list includes all weapons and rolls.

It will "Thumbs Up" weapons with at least 1 desired perk as indicated in Aegis's Spreadsheet.

You can ten compare dupes by how many perks they have tagged.

Tier A-F and notes as also included i the triage on weapon inspection.

## Why use this
I am a bit of a hoarder in Destiny 2. I like to have 1 copy of each gun with a good roll on it, even if they are not very good right now.

With this wishlist, you can find the best perks even on bad guns while still being able to tell if the gun is A tier or F tier.
