## How to Use
Copy a link from the list below, or suggest one for me to add

In DIM go to Settings->Wish List

Clear the wishlist of the current included rolls (If you want)

Under "Add another wish list" paste the link

## How the list works
Unlike some other lists, the Base list includes all weapons and rolls.

It will "Thumbs Up" weapons with at least 1 desired perk as indicated in Aegis's Spreadsheet.

You can then compare dupes by how many perks they have tagged.

Tier A-F and notes are also included in the triage on weapon inspection.

The more refined lists simply limit which weapons are included for those looking to clean their vault up.

The expanded view of weapons may appear strange due to the limitations of DIM and the desire to flag multiple perks in a single column. I recommend simply relying on the "Thumbs Up" indicators to identify well rolled items.

## Why use this
I am a bit of a hoarder in Destiny 2. I like to have 1 copy of each gun with a good roll on it, even if they are not very good right now.

With this wishlist, you can find the best perks even on bad guns while still being able to tell if the gun is A tier or F tier.
